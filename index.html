🩺 About Us – Prenatal Atlas

👩‍⚕️ Who We Are: Prenatal Atlas is a comprehensive web-based application designed to visually track and analyze fetal and embryonic development through AI-powered 3D models, gestational timelines, and medical reports. Developed by a multidisciplinary team from Cleveland State University, this platform supports prenatal care by combining cutting-edge visualization, personalized health monitoring, and secure data analytics. Whether you are a healthcare provider, expecting parent, medical student, or researcher, Prenatal Atlas provides accessible, real-time tools for observing fetal health and development from fertilization to delivery.

🎯 Our Mission: Our mission is to empower expecting parents and medical professionals with clear, interactive, and scientifically validated insights into prenatal development. By making complex biological information more visual and accessible, we aim to support early diagnosis, better patient engagement, and informed decision-making.

🔬 Our Vision: To redefine prenatal healthcare education and monitoring through intelligent visualization, and become the trusted digital reference for fetal development worldwide.

👥 Meet the Team Behind Prenatal Atlas: Our team consists of medical advisors and OB-GYN experts who validate the biological accuracy of all visualizations and reports. Our AI/ML engineers and developers ensure advanced, real-time analytics and automation, while our UX designers and frontend developers focus on creating intuitive and accessible interfaces. Additionally, health informatics and regulatory consultants guide platform compliance with healthcare data laws.

💡 Key Features of Prenatal Atlas:

1. Interactive 3D Visualization: Users can explore high-resolution, zoomable 3D fetal and embryonic models, from pre-embryonic stages through to full-term development.

2. AI-Driven Growth Analysis: AI algorithms trained on 15+ years of medical imaging data provide predictive analysis, flag anomalies, and generate weekly reports tailored to the gestational stage.

3. Personalized Growth Tracker: Expecting parents and doctors can input key metrics (gestational age, fetal weight, maternal vitals) to generate individualized development charts.

4. Gestational Timeline & Milestones: Dynamic timeline interface that details every key developmental phase, from zygote to full-term fetus, including organogenesis and physiological changes.

5. Medical Education & Research Tools: Interactive modules for use in classrooms and labs, including video lessons, printable diagrams, and annotated simulations of key biological processes.

6. Real-Time Data Automation: Automated integration with ultrasound devices, fetal monitors, or user-entered data to generate updated visuals and reports without manual effort.

📋 Rules and Regulations (Compliance & Use Policy):

🛡️ 1. Medical Disclaimer: Prenatal Atlas is intended for educational and preliminary observational purposes only. It is not a substitute for professional medical advice, diagnosis, or emergency care. Any concerns or abnormal results generated by AI must be reviewed by a licensed healthcare provider before action is taken.

🔐 2. Data Privacy & Security Compliance: Prenatal Atlas is designed with security-by-design principles, ensuring privacy and data protection in line with global laws. All personal health data (PHI) is encrypted in transit and at rest (AES-256 bit). Explicit consent is required before any personal data collection, and Prenatal Atlas complies with HIPAA (U.S.) and GDPR (EU) regulations.

✅ 3. User Eligibility and Usage Policy: Users must be 18 years or older, or have parental/guardian supervision. Professionals using this platform must be licensed healthcare providers in their country. Misrepresentation of data, including inputting false gestational details, is prohibited and monitored via backend validation.

📏 4. Content Standards and Use: All visualizations and educational modules are peer-reviewed by medical experts. Any user-generated content must adhere to platform guidelines and not include false health claims, offensive content, or violate user privacy.

💳 5. Licensing, Payments & Access: Prenatal Atlas offers a freemium model, where basic features are free, and premium features require a subscription. Institutional users (clinics, universities) may request volume licenses for EHR integration. Subscriptions are covered by a 7-day money-back policy unless AI-generated reports have been downloaded.

⚙️ 6. Security & Data Integrity: Prenatal Atlas is hosted on HIPAA-compliant cloud platforms, with bi-weekly penetration testing and annual security audits. Two-Factor Authentication (2FA) is enforced for sensitive health data access.

🧾 7. Regulatory Oversight & Future Certification: Prenatal Atlas intends to undergo certifications including ISO/IEC 27001 for Information Security Management and CE Marking for European compliance.

Designed for expecting parents, doctors, and educators, it offers full data security, regulatory compliance, and medical accuracy.
